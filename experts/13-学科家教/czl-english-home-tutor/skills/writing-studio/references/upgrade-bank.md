# 升格句库

> 升格不是换大词，是**换视角**：把形容词换成动作，把评价换成场景，把结论换成细节。

## 一、情绪：写身体，不写形容词

| 原句 | 升格 | 为什么 |
|---|---|---|
| He was very nervous. | His fingers kept curling around the edge of the ticket. | 紧张变成了一个可见的动作，读者自己得出结论 |
| She was very happy. | She pressed the letter to her chest and laughed out loud. | 两个动作替代"happy"，还带出声音 |
| He was very angry. | He set the cup down harder than he meant to. | "比预想更用力"暗示失控，不点破情绪 |
| She was very tired. | She leaned against the doorframe and let her bag slide to the floor. | 包滑落这个细节比"tired"更有分量 |
| He was very sad. | He kept staring at the empty chair. | 用"空椅子"指向失去的人 |
| I was very surprised. | I read the message twice before I understood it. | 重复阅读＝不敢相信 |
| He was afraid. | He counted his steps so he would not have to look back. | 用"不敢回头"写恐惧 |

## 二、场景：五感只选一个

| 原句 | 升格 | 为什么 |
|---|---|---|
| The room was very quiet. | The clock on the wall sounded louder than usual. | 用声音写安静 |
| The rain was very heavy. | Rain drummed on the tin roof all night. | 拟声＋材质，画面立起来 |
| The street was very crowded. | Shoulders, bags and elbows pushed me forward. | 不用"crowded"，用身体挤压 |
| It was very cold. | My breath hung in the air before it disappeared. | 呼出的白气是冷的证据 |
| The sky was very dark. | Clouds swallowed the last light over the hills. | 一个动词吞掉天光 |

## 三、动作：精准动词替掉"walk / look / say"

| 原句 | 升格 | 为什么 |
|---|---|---|
| He walked slowly into the room. | He edged into the room. | edge＝贴着边挪，带犹豫 |
| She looked at the paper carefully. | She ran her finger down the list, line by line. | 具体到手指和顺序 |
| He ran very fast. | He shot past me, one hand still holding his cap. | 速度＋一个随身细节 |
| She said nothing. | She opened her mouth, then closed it. | 想说又咽回去，比"沉默"信息多 |

## 四、应用文：把态度落到事上

| 原句 | 升格 | 为什么 |
|---|---|---|
| I think this activity is very good. | I find this activity practical: it gave us a reason to speak English outside class. | 评价后面必须跟理由 |
| I hope you can come. | I hope you can make it — your presence would mean a lot to the team. | 期待＋对方的价值 |
| Please reply as soon as possible. | I would appreciate a reply before Friday so we can finalize the schedule. | 给时间点，给原因 |
| We should protect the environment. | We can start with one thing: bring your own bottle to every class. | 口号换成动作 |

## 五、四个可迁移句型（换主语就能用）

| 句型 | 例 | 用在哪 |
|---|---|---|
| Not until ... did ... | **Not until** the lights went out **did** we realize how late it was. | 续写转折、强调时间 |
| What struck me most was ... | **What struck me most was** the way she listened without interrupting. | 投稿、演讲稿提炼 |
| It was then that ... | **It was then that** I understood why he had kept the old map. | 续写收束、回扣伏笔 |
| Had it not been for ... | **Had it not been for** your note, I would have given up. | 感谢信、演讲致谢 |

## 六、三条禁忌

1. **不堆大词**。`utilize`、`commence`、`approximately` 用在 80 词的应用文里，只会显得别扭。用 `use`、`start`、`about`。
2. **一句一个比喻**。一段里出现三个"像……一样"，读者会累，而且容易写错。
3. **不背整篇模板**。背结构可以，背整篇会在开头第一句就露出来——阅卷老师一天看几百份，同一句见过太多次。

## 七、升格练习怎么布置

一次只练一种：

1. 给三个"very + 形容词"的句子，全部改成动作。
2. 给一个场景（考场发卷前 30 秒），只写三句，必须有一句是声音。
3. 把自己上周作文里最平的一句挑出来，改一遍，说明为什么更好。

**改完必须自己说一遍"为什么更好"**，说不出来就是没学会。
