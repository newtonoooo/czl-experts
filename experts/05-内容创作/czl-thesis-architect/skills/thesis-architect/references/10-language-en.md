# W10 英文润色与中译英

产出：改后英文段落 + 改动对照 + 术语一致性检查。

## 一、中国作者写英文论文的八个高频病

| # | 病 | 例子 | 改法 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 空转开头 | It is worth noting that the results indicate… | The results indicate… |
| 2 | 名词化堆叠 | the implementation of the optimization of the system | optimizing the system |
| 3 | 过度被动 | It was found by the authors that… | We found that… / The analysis found… |
| 4 | 中文语序直译 | This study takes three schools as the research object | This study examines three schools |
| 5 | 悬垂修饰 | Based on the data, the conclusion is drawn… | Based on the data, we conclude… |
| 6 | 连词滥用 | Moreover… Furthermore… Additionally…（连续三段） | 逻辑强才用，否则靠句序 |
| 7 | 时态混乱 | 方法与结果用现在时，结论用过去时 | 方法/结果过去时；结论现在时 |
| 8 | 冠词与单复数 | a research, informations, the society | research（不可数）, information, society |

## 二、动词要实，不要虚

| 虚 | 实 |
|---|---|
| make a contribution to | contribute to |
| conduct an investigation of | investigate |
| give an explanation for | explain |
| have an influence on | affect / influence |
| is indicative of | indicates |
| play an important role in | drive / shape / constrain（选一个准确的） |

一句话里最多一个 `play a role`。学术写作里这个词组九成是没想清楚机制。

## 三、连接词精简表

| 关系 | 首选 | 慎用 |
|---|---|---|
| 递进 | further, moreover（一段一次） | additionally, in addition（连续用显机械） |
| 转折 | however, yet, in contrast | nevertheless（过度正式） |
| 因果 | therefore, thus, consequently | hence（少量）、so（口语） |
| 并列 | while, whereas（对比并列） | meanwhile（时间并列才用） |
| 举例 | for example, such as | e.g.（正文少用，括号内可） |

## 四、Hedging 分级（与证据匹配）

| 证据强度 | 动词 | 句式 |
|---|---|---|
| 强 | demonstrate, establish, show | The results demonstrate that… |
| 中 | suggest, indicate, point to | The pattern suggests that… |
| 弱 | may, might, appears to, is likely to | This may reflect… |

自查：结论句里出现 `prove`、`confirm`、`definitely`，先问证据够不够。单一样本的相关分析只能写 suggest。

## 五、中译英的五个动作

1. **拆句**：中文一句常含三个分句，英文拆成主句 + 从句/分词。
2. **换主语**：中文爱用"通过……，发现了……"（无主语），英文要有明确主语（We / The analysis / This study）。
3. **动词优先**：中文的名词短语（"对……的分析"）→ 英文动词（analyze）。
4. **信息重排**：英文的旧信息在前、新信息在后；中文常反过来。
5. **去重复**：中文四字词连用（"积极稳妥、扎实推进"）译成一个准确的动词。

示例：

- 中文："本研究以三所县域初中为对象，通过问卷与访谈相结合的方式，对课后服务参与率的影响因素进行了分析。"
- ✗ 直译："This study takes three county junior high schools as objects, through the combination of questionnaire and interview, conducts an analysis of the influencing factors of after-school service participation rate."
- ✓ 改写："Drawing on questionnaires and interviews in three county junior high schools, this study examines what drives participation in after-school programs."

## 六、十五组对照

| # | 原文 | 改后 |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | With the development of society, after-school services become more and more important. | Since 2021, all 27 public junior high schools in the county have offered after-school programs. |
| 2 | This paper mainly discusses the influence of commuting time on participation rate. | This paper examines how commuting time affects participation. |
| 3 | The reason is because of the policy. | The reason is the policy design. |
| 4 | There are many students who participate in the program. | Many students participate in the program. |
| 5 | It is very important to note that the sample is small. | The sample is small (n = 312), which limits precision. |
| 6 | We can see from Figure 4-1 that… | Figure 4-1 shows that… |
| 7 | The participation rate is higher than before. | Participation rose from 48.7% to 64.5%. |
| 8 | In a certain extent, this proves our hypothesis. | This is consistent with our hypothesis. |
| 9 | The data is analyzed by using SPSS. | Data were analyzed in SPSS 26. |
| 10 | More and more researchers pay attention to this issue. | Twelve studies since 2018 have addressed this issue (L1–L12). |
| 11 | This study has some limitations. | Three limitations qualify these findings. |
| 12 | Due to the limitation of time and energy, we did not do longitudinal research. | Time constraints precluded a longitudinal design. |
| 13 | The result is significantly different. | The difference was significant (F(2, 309) = 24.7, p < .001, η² = 0.14). |
| 14 | We should pay more attention to this problem. | Program hours should be aligned with parents' commuting schedules. |
| 15 | In the future, further research is needed. | Future work should track the same students over time to identify causal direction. |

## 七、示范段落（润色前后）

**原文（典型中式学术英语）**

> With the continuous development of education reform, after-school service has been paid more and more
> attention by the society. In this paper, through the questionnaire survey of three schools, the influencing
> factors of participation rate are analyzed. It is found that there is a negative correlation between commuting
> time and participation rate, and the family income has no significant influence. This shows that the commuting
> time is very important. Therefore, schools should pay more attention to this problem.

**改后**

> Participation in after-school programs has drawn growing policy attention since 2021, yet little is known
> about why it varies across schools. Using parent surveys from three county junior high schools (n = 312),
> this study examines the correlates of participation. Commuting time was negatively associated with weekly
> participation (r = −0.38, p < .001), whereas household income showed no significant effect (β = 0.06, p = .31).
> The pattern suggests that time constraints, rather than affordability, shape participation in county schools—
> a mechanism that program schedules could address directly.

改动说明：拆长句（1 句→3 句）、去被动（has been paid attention by→drew attention）、
给数字（negative correlation→r = −0.38）、去空评价（very important→suggests that…）、
结论落到可操作（pay more attention→program schedules could address）。

## 八、句式库

**起段**

- "Drawing on …, this study examines …"
- "Despite growing interest in X, little is known about Y."
- "Prior work has established X; less clear is Y."

**引出发现**

- "Participation varied substantially across schools, ranging from 48.7% to 82.1%."
- "Commuting time, but not income, predicted participation."

**解释**

- "One interpretation is that …"
- "This pattern is consistent with the view that …"
- "An alternative account—that …—receives little support: …"

**收口**

- "These findings suggest that …"
- "The results should be read with two caveats: …"
- "Whether this holds beyond county schools remains an open question."

## 九、交付格式

```
一、改后英文（可直接替换）
二、改动对照（5—12 条：原文 / 改后 / 为什么）
三、术语表（中英对照，全文统一用这套）
四、需你确认的表述（涉及你的数据或判断）
```

## 十、不要做的事

- 不要把中文的成语、口号直译成英文（"扎实推进"→ advance steadily 是废话，直接说做了什么）。
- 不要为了"学术感"堆被动和长名词短语。
- 不要用 `obviously`、`clearly`、`of course`——审稿人会问"真的显然吗"。
- 不要在摘要里出现引用标记（多数期刊不允许）。
