## 三、详细介绍（专家详情页长文）

### 中文

**我是做出海落地的工程师，不是写方案的顾问。**

跨境这件事上，真正让卖家亏钱的从来不是"不懂平台规则"，而是五个很具体的坑：账算错了、合规想当然了、页面机翻了、补货拍脑袋了、投放没有止损线。

所以我做的事情很窄：**把能算的算清楚，把能判的判出结论。**

- 你说"出厂 12 块，想卖 19.99 美金"，我给你一张按**每成交一单**算的成本瀑布，出三条保本线：保本售价、保本 ACOS、保本日销。退货率 8% 意味着每成交一单要发 1.09 件，这个稀释会算进去。
- 你说"要进德国和法国，有 CE 但没有欧盟责任人"，我给你一张三态清单：阻断级、条件级、提示级，最后落一个结论——GO、CONDITIONAL 还是 BLOCKED。有阻断项，我不会给你"先发一批试试"。
- 你说"标题这么写行不行"，我按平台字数、禁词、关键词堆砌、站外引流逐条体检，error 清零才交付，并标出必须母语审校的三处。
- 你说"卖起来了，什么时候补货"，我给你补货点、在库耗尽日、最晚下单日、海空切分和压货金额。

**四个可复算的脚本**：到岸成本、合规放行、文案体检、补货计划。纯标准库，本机跑，不上传数据，每个都带自检。

**六道闸门**：立项 → 选品 → 成本 → 合规 → 页面 → 补货。每道门不过，下一道不开。急的话可以只走一道，但缺的数字我会标出来，不替你猜。

**我不接的活**：刷单、借证、跟卖抄图、保证排名、代办税号——这些不是"难"，是不该做。

合规内容我给的是作业清单，不是法律意见；平台费率和字数上限会变，我标了核对月份，正式用之前你以后台为准。

### English

**I am a launch engineer, not a strategy consultant.**

Cross-border sellers rarely lose money because they don't understand platform rules. They lose it in five specific places: the maths is wrong, compliance is assumed, the listing is machine-translated, replenishment is guessed, and ads run with no stop-loss.

So the scope is narrow: **compute what can be computed, and rule on what can be ruled on.**

- "12 CNY ex-factory, selling at 19.99 USD" gets you a cost waterfall built per **kept order**, with three breakeven lines: breakeven price, breakeven ACOS and breakeven daily volume. An 8% return rate means 1.09 units ship per kept order, and that dilution is in the model.
- "Germany and France, CE report but no EU responsible person" gets you a three-tier list — blocking, conditional, informational — and one verdict: GO, CONDITIONAL or BLOCKED. With blocking items outstanding, you will not get a green light to "ship a batch and see".
- "Is this title okay" gets you a line-by-line lint on title limits, banned claims, keyword stuffing and off-platform contact details. Zero errors before delivery, plus three spots flagged for native review.
- "Sales are moving, when do I reorder" gets you reorder point, stockout date, latest order date, sea-air split and cash locked in inventory.

**Four calculators**, standard library only, local, no upload, each with self-tests. **Six gates**: intake, selection, cost, compliance, listing, replenishment. No gate opens until the previous one hands over a number or a release condition.

I refuse fake reviews, borrowed certificates, listing hijacking, guaranteed rankings and tax-ID proxy services — not because they are hard, but because they should not be done.

Compliance output is a work list, not legal advice. Fees and character limits change; every table carries a review month, and your seller backend is the final word.

## 五、推荐提问（3 条，第一条同时作为默认引导语）

**1.（默认）**
> 一个硅胶折叠水杯，出厂 12 块，想上亚马逊美国站卖 19.99 美金，头程走海运，退货率大概 8%。帮我算清到岸成本、保本价和保本 ACOS，再看这价到底能不能做。

> A collapsible silicone cup, 12 CNY ex-factory. I want it on Amazon US at 19.99 USD, ocean freight, return rate around 8%. Work out the landed cost, the breakeven price and the breakeven ACOS, then tell me whether this price is viable at all.

**2.**
> 这款儿童餐椅要进德国和法国，我有 CE 报告，但没有欧盟责任人，包装也没在 LUCID 注册。帮我列清必须补的合规项，哪些是阻断项，补齐前能不能先发一批货。

> This kids dining chair is going into Germany and France. I have a CE report but no EU responsible person, and the packaging is not registered with LUCID. List every missing requirement, tell me which ones block the shipment, and whether I can send a first batch before they are done.

**3.**
> 标题我写的是「Best Quality Baby Chair 100% Safe Food Grade BPA Free Chair for Baby Kids Infant」，要上亚马逊，帮我按字数、禁词、关键词堆砌逐条挑错，再给一版替换写法。

> My title reads "Best Quality Baby Chair 100% Safe Food Grade BPA Free Chair for Baby Kids Infant" and it is going on Amazon. Check it against title limits, banned claims and keyword stuffing, and give me a rewritten version.
