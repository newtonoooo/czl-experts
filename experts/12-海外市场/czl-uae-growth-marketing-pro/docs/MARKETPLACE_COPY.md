## 5. 详细介绍（详情页正文）

### 中文

**它做什么**

把一个营销目标变成一份能直接派活的投放方案：钱怎么分、投给谁、什么时候投、话怎么说、哪里会踩雷。

**四个核心能力**

1. **人群分层**——阿联酋不是一个市场。它按本国公民、阿拉伯外籍、南亚外籍、欧美外籍、游客五层切开，每层给语言、主媒介、决策驱动和敏感点，再据此决定预算密度。
2. **渠道预算建模**——内置确定性模型，把预算、目标、人群、行业、城市转成渠道配比表，分配精确到迪拉姆，且不会低于任何渠道的起投门槛。每个渠道标注角色（信任/发现/转化）、KPI 形态和素材要求。
3. **节点排期**——绑定斋月、开斋节、宰牲节、国庆日、迪拜购物节、开学季的真实节奏，自动倒推素材锁定、预算锁定、上线预热三个里程碑，并把截止日回退到工作日。
4. **合规与双语文案**——交付前扫描宗教、政治王室、性别身体、酒精赌博、绝对化用语、价格折扣、金融收益、医疗健康、教育承诺、文化适配十一类风险，给出替代表达，并输出阿拉伯语与英语双版本。

**它怎么工作**

五段式 OASIS 框架：目标校验 → 人群分层 → 预算建模 → 节点排期 → 合规与文案，最后过八项质检门。缺预算或目标会先停下来问，不硬编。

**它不做什么**

不承诺 ROI 或具体转化数字；不编造案例与官方合作；不代替法律意见。市场数据为建模区间，对外使用前需以平台实时报价与官方统计复核。

### English

**What it does**

Turns a marketing goal into a media plan you can hand to a project manager on Monday morning: where the money goes, who it targets, when it runs, what it says, and where it breaks.

**Four capabilities**

1. **Audience tiering** — the UAE is not one market. It splits the population into five tiers (Emirati nationals, Arab expatriates, South Asian expatriates, Western expatriates, tourists) and gives each one its languages, primary platforms, decision drivers and sensitivities, then sizes budget density accordingly.
2. **Channel budget modelling** — a deterministic model turns budget, objective, audience, sector and city into an allocation table that sums exactly to the budget and never under-funds a channel below its own minimum spend. Every channel carries a role (trust / discovery / conversion), a KPI shape and creative requirements.
3. **Calendar scheduling** — anchored to Ramadan, both Eids, UAE National Day, Dubai Shopping Festival and the school return, with backward milestones for asset lock, budget lock and warm-up, and deadlines pushed off the weekend.
4. **Compliance and bilingual copy** — screens copy against eleven risk categories before delivery, proposes approved alternatives, and returns Arabic and English versions.

**How it works**

A five-stage OASIS framework: objective check → audience tiering → spend modelling → calendar → compliance and copy, gated by an eight-point QA checklist. It stops and asks when budget or objective is missing rather than inventing one.

**What it does not do**

It does not promise ROI or conversion numbers, does not invent case studies or official partnerships, and is not a substitute for legal advice. Market figures are modelling ranges and must be re-verified against live platform quotes and official statistics.
