## 三、详细介绍（详情页用）

### 中文

做沙特市场，最贵的不是试错，是拿着一张没做完的功课去见了一个不该见的人，然后被一个不能兑现的承诺绑三年。

这个专家不给你讲沙特有多大机会，它做的是六件事，一件一件逼出结论：

**G0 立案** — 把「我们想进沙特」压成一张立案卡：卖给谁、以什么身份进、预算多少、多久要第一单。缺信息只问三个问题。

**G1 准入** — 三线并行判定：外资能不能做、要哪些行业许可、货能不能进。结论只有三种：可以做、有条件可以做、不能做。不能做的时候，它不会陪你谈代理，只会给替代路径。

**G2 路径** — 独资、合资、代理、技术许可、区域总部、经济特区，六条路算三年账，给出一个推荐、一个备选，以及被排除路径的理由。

**G3 渠道** — 先建至少六家的长名单，再用八维评分筛到三家以内，红旗项一票否决。它会明确告诉你哪一家不能签，以及为什么。

**G4 投标** — 对照 Etimad 的资格清单逐项打勾，判断该投、补格后再投、还是不投。劝退也是结论。

**G5 落地** — 把决定变成带责任人的步骤链，按有效商务周折算时间（斋月、两个节日、夏季各扣一段），每类风险配一条可执行的止损线。

**它和「会聊沙特」的工具不一样的地方：**

- 有闸门：上一道没结论，不往下走。
- 有工具：五个零依赖脚本做准入规则匹配、三年账、伙伴评分、商机排序，算得出来、可复核。
- 有红线：代持、挂名、虚假本地化、规避制裁、非正规佣金一律不接，并给合法替代。
- 有纪律：每个数字只有三种状态——有来源、区间估计、待核实。没有第四种。

**适合**：制造商海外负责人、贸易与品牌方、工程与系统集成商、看项目的投资团队、已落地但业务没起来的属地负责人。

**不适合**：想要「沙特市场概况 PPT」的；想要保证中标或保证获批的；想找规避监管方案的。

### 英文

Entering Saudi Arabia rarely fails because the sector was wrong. It fails because someone walked into the wrong meeting with unfinished homework and left bound to a promise nobody could keep.

This expert does not sell you the size of the opportunity. It runs six gates and refuses to move on until each one returns a verdict:

**G0 Intake** — compresses "we want to enter Saudi" into a case card: who buys, in what capacity, what budget, first order by when. If information is missing it asks three questions, once.

**G1 Entry gate** — three lines in parallel: can foreign capital do this, which sector licences are required, can the goods clear. Verdicts are GO, CONDITIONAL or BLOCKED. On BLOCKED it will not discuss distributors; it gives alternative routes only.

**G2 Route** — wholly foreign-owned entity, joint venture, agency, licensing, regional headquarters, special economic zone: a three-year account for each, one recommendation, one fallback, and written reasons for what was excluded.

**G3 Channel** — build a long list of at least six, score on eight dimensions, cut to three, and let red flags veto. It will tell you which candidate must not be signed, and why.

**G4 Tender fit** — walk the Etimad qualification checklist item by item and decide bid, fix-then-bid, or no-bid. A recommendation not to bid is a legitimate deliverable.

**G5 Mobilization** — turn the decision into a step chain with owners, convert the calendar into effective business weeks (Ramadan, two Eids and summer deducted), and attach an executable stop-loss to every risk.

**What makes it different:** gates instead of topics; five dependency-free calculators instead of adjectives; hard red lines on nominee arrangements, fake Saudization and irregular commissions; and a numeric discipline where every figure is sourced, ranged, or explicitly marked unverified.
