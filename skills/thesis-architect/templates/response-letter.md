# 审稿意见回复模板

> 逐点回复。每条三段：复述意见 → 做了什么 → 核验说明。

## 结构

```
Response to Reviewers

Manuscript: [题目]
Manuscript ID: [编号]

We thank the editor and the reviewers for their careful reading. Our responses follow each
comment in turn; changes are marked in the revised manuscript with [tracked changes / blue text]
and page numbers refer to the revised version.

--- Reviewer 1 ---

Comment 1.1: [原文照录，不改写、不弱化]
Response: We thank the reviewer for this point. We have [具体改动], now in Section 3.2, p. 3,
paragraph 2, and added Table 3-1 comparing the three schools on region, size, and staffing.
We confirm that this addition does not change our conclusions; it improves reproducibility only.

Comment 1.2: ...
Response: ...

--- Reviewer 2 ---

Comment 2.1: [原文照录]
Response: We agree that [承认合理之处]. We have [改动] in Section 5.4, p. 18.
[若涉及不采纳，见下]
```

## 不采纳的写法

```
Comment 2.3: The authors should add longitudinal data.
Response: We thank the reviewer for this suggestion. The present study uses a cross-sectional
design and cannot provide longitudinal data. We have made this limitation explicit in Section 5.4
(p. 18), including its implications for causal inference. A longitudinal follow-up is now stated
as the next step in Section 6.3 (p. 21).
```

正当理由三种：超出研究设计范围、数据不可得、与本文问题无关。
**"工作量太大"不是理由。**

## 五个禁忌

1. 只回摘要，漏具体条目（审稿人会逐条核）。
2. 用"已在文中相应修改"含糊过去。
3. 争辩语气（"审稿人显然误解了"）→ 改成"我们表述不清，已改写"。
4. 改了但没标位置。
5. 承诺"已全面修改"实际只改一处。

## 交付格式

```
一、回复信全文（可直接提交）
二、修改位置表
三、仍需你确认的两处（涉及数据或判断）
四、若审稿人坚持不接受，备选方案（两条）
```
